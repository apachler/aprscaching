# APRScaching

**Find real places on the air.** APRScaching is a geocaching game for radio amateurs, with a ham-radio
**Shack** around it. You hide a cache, go and find it, and log the find. Where a radio station heard you near
the cache, the find counts as verified by radio. It runs in a browser, self-hosts on a Raspberry Pi, and links
up with other instances into one open network.

!!! tip "New here?"
    Sign in at **[aprscaching.net](https://aprscaching.net)** with your callsign and log your first find. There
    is nothing to install. [Your first find](play/first-find.md) walks you through it in five minutes.

Instances form one network with no central server: caches, finds and find confirmations travel between them
as signed records, and each sysop decides whom to trust ([How federation works](run/federation/how-it-works.md)).
