# Heritage places

This page is for players. It explains the five heritage types: SOTA summits, POTA parks, WWFF reserves,
bunkers and castles. They are real places from award programmes and maps, and you log them like caches.

## What it is

A heritage place is a summit, park, reserve, bunker or castle that your [sysop](../../glossary.md#sysop)
imported onto the map. There is no container. The place itself is the cache, and its code is the programme's
own reference.

| Type | What it is | Imported from | Shown as owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SOTA summit** | A Summits on the Air summit | SOTA, by region | SOTA |
| **POTA park** | A Parks on the Air park | POTA, by location | POTA |
| **WWFF reserve** | A WWFF nature reserve | WWFF, by programme | WWFF |
| **Bunker** | A bunker landmark | WWBOTA and UKBOTA | WWBOTA |
| **Castle** | A castle landmark | OpenStreetMap or Wikidata, when the sysop marks the places as castles | OSM or Wikidata |

The cache sheet shows **imported · SOTA** (or the other source) beside the code, and **Imported from SOTA ·
view source ↗** with a link to the place's page at its source. When two sources list the same place within
100 m, the map shows it once, and the amateur-radio programme's entry wins.

## How you find it

1. Open the place and read its source page for access and opening times.
2. Tap **Navigate** to take the coordinates to your maps app.
3. Go to the place: the summit, the park, the reserve, the bunker or the castle.
4. Log the find while you are there.

Turn on **Activity spots** in **Search & filter** to see live SOTA and POTA activations. When an activation is
within 300 m of a place, its cache sheet shows **Being activated now by** the activator's callsign, with band
and mode.

## What "found" means

You were at the place's pin when you logged. The game checks your position against the pin, within 150 m plus
your phone's accuracy. A summit's or park's pin is one point, so log near it, not anywhere in a large park.

## How it is logged and verified

Log it like any cache: **✓ Log a find**, **Couldn't find it** or **Add a note**, in the app or from your radio.
A find can reach **Radio-verified**, **Location-verified** or **Logged**. See
[Traditional caches](traditional.md#how-it-is-logged-and-verified) for the rules.

A heritage find scores points like any other find. Imported places have a difficulty and terrain of 1.5. Your
finds on them stay on this instance: other instances never get them. A find here is an APRScaching find, and
it is not sent to the SOTA, POTA or WWFF logs.

A verified find on a heritage place earns its badge:

| Type | Badge |
|---|---|
| SOTA summit | **Summiteer** |
| POTA park | **Park hunter** |
| WWFF reserve | **Flora & fauna** |
| Bunker | **Bunker hunter** |
| Castle | **Castle hunter** |

By radio, send the place's reference as its code, for example `FOUND OE/ST-001` for a SOTA summit or
`FOUND OEFF-0123` for a WWFF reserve. Upper or lower case both work.

## What a hider sets

Nothing: the sysop imports heritage places, and running the import again updates them. To get places of a
region onto the map, ask your sysop. They can follow
[Import heritage places](../../run/day-to-day/import-places.md).

A player cannot hide a heritage place: the hide form leaves the types out, and the instance refuses them from
anyone but the sysop. A cache on a summit is hidden as a traditional cache, and a find on it earns no
**Summiteer** badge.

## Example

**OE/ST-123 · Hochkogel** · SOTA summit · owner SOTA · imported · SOTA.
OE8APR-7 hikes to the top, beacons from the summit, and logs the find in the app. A receiving station of the
instance hears the beacon, so the find is **Radio-verified** and earns **Summiteer**.

## Tips

- Check the source page first. Bunkers and castles can be closed, fenced or on private ground.
- On a summit, beacon your position before you log. If a receiving station of the instance hears it, the find
  can be **Radio-verified**.
- If you activate the place for SOTA or POTA, log the activation with that programme as usual. The
  APRScaching find is separate.

## Map marker

Each type has its own pin colour and symbol. The Phosphor theme swaps some symbols.

| Type | Pin colour | Symbol | Phosphor symbol |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOTA summit | grey | triangle (▲) | triangle (▲) |
| POTA park | clay | sun (❂) | club (♣) |
| WWFF reserve | forest green | flower (❀) | spade (♠) |
| Bunker | grey | square in a square (▣) | filled square (■) |
| Castle | clay | chess rook (♜) | hash (#) |
