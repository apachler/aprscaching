# Community

This page explains what other players see of your hunting, and what you see of theirs: ratings, favourites,
alerts, badges and ranks. It is for every player.

## Ratings

A cache page shows its **Rating**: the average of 1 to 5 stars and the number of ratings. You rate with the
stars under it. Tap again to change your rating; each callsign has one rating per cache.

The owner decides who may rate when they hide the cache:

| **Who can rate** | Who may rate |
|---|---|
| **Finders only** (default) | A callsign with a verified find on this cache |
| **Anyone signed in** | Every signed-in player |
| **Nobody (disabled)** | Nobody |

A verified find means a **Radio-verified** or **Location-verified** find
([How finds are verified](verification.md)). If you may not rate, the page says *Log a verified find to rate
this cache* or *Sign in to rate*.

## Favourites

The heart at the top of a cache page marks it as a favourite. The number beside it counts everyone who did.
Tap it again to take your favourite back.

## Alerts and the watchlist

The watchlist watches callsigns, not caches. Add a friend's callsign and you get an alert when the network
hears it.

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Under **Watchlist**, type the callsign in **Watch a callsign**, for example `OE8APR`, and tap **Watch**.
   The callsign shows as a chip. Tap the chip to stop watching.

You get at most one alert an hour for each callsign. When the station is about 500 m or less from a cache, the
alert names the cache.

Alerts show under **Alerts** in the same group. Two more ways to get them:

- **Browser push**: tap **Enable**. The instance must offer push, and on an iPhone you install the app to
  your home screen first.
- **Email digest**: alerts batched into an email. It needs an email on your account.

The same alerts list the finds on your caches and the finds your station verified. They also tell you about
cache adoption ([Cache adoption](hide-a-cache.md#cache-adoption)).

## Badges

Badges show on your profile under **You**. Hold a pointer over a badge to see how you earned it. Only
verified finds count, each cache once.

| Badge | How you earn it |
|---|---|
| **First find** | Your first verified find |
| **10 finds** | 10 verified finds |
| **50 finds** | 50 verified finds |
| **100 finds** | 100 verified finds |
| **500 finds** | 500 verified finds |
| **Radio-verified** | One **Radio-verified** find |
| **Summiteer** | A verified find on a SOTA summit |
| **Park hunter** | A verified find on a POTA park |
| **Rover hunter** | A verified find on a living cache |
| **Flora & fauna** | A verified find on a WWFF reserve |
| **Castle hunter** | A verified find on a castle |
| **Bunker hunter** | A verified find on a bunker |
| **Hider** | Your first hidden cache |
| **Cache architect** | 5 hidden caches |
| **Cache master** | 20 hidden caches |

Hide badges count the caches you own that are not archived. A badge stays once you have it.

## Ranks

**Ranks** shows the leaderboard for the area on your map. Tap **Points** or **Finds** to sort it, and
**All time**, **Year** or **Month** to count every find, the last 365 days or the last 30 days. Move the map
and tap **↻ this area** to load a new area. Tap a callsign to see that player's finds, points, hides and
badges.

Each cache you found counts once, with your best find on it. Its points are:

| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| **Radio-verified** find | 10 |
| **Location-verified** find | 5 |
| Difficulty | 1 to 5 |
| Terrain | 1 to 5 |

So a **Location-verified** find on a cache with difficulty 2 and terrain 1.5 earns 5 + 2 + 1.5 = 8.5 points.

Only verified finds by verified callsigns reach the board
([Verify your callsign](join.md#verify-your-callsign)). A **Logged** find is not verified, so it earns no
points and no badges. Your profile counts your verified finds and points even before your callsign is
verified.

Finds you log under an SSID, such as `OE8APR-7`, count for your base callsign. Owners don't log their own
caches as found, so a cache never earns its owner points.

**Activity** shows recent finds in your area, the **Top finders**, and the **Top corroborators**. A
corroborator is a receiving station whose radio heard finds on the air and made them **Radio-verified**
([corroboration](../glossary.md#corroboration)). If your station did that, your profile shows **Infrastructure**
with the number of finds it corroborated.

When two living caches meet, both record a rendezvous. It shows on the cache page and earns no points
([Living caches](cache-types/living.md)).

## Share and export

- On a cache page, **Copy link** copies a link to it. **QR** shows a QR code to print and place at your
  station or the site; **download SVG** saves it.
- On the map, **Search & filter → Share this view** copies a link to what you see.
- Under **You**, **Embed your badge** shows an image of your all-time rank, verified finds, points and hides.
  **Copy** copies the HTML that shows it on QRZ.com, in a forum signature or on a club page. The image stays
  current.
- The instance offers caches as GPX (for GPS units) and KML (for map programs), and your finds as ADIF (for
  your logbook program). The app has no download button for them yet. Logbook and GPS tools can fetch them
  from the instance's [public read API](../reference/api.md#public-read-api).
