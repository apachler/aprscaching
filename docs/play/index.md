# What is APRScaching?

APRScaching is a treasure hunt for radio amateurs. Someone **hides a cache**: a spot on the map tied to
amateur radio, such as a summit, a park, a castle or a bench with a view. You **go there and log the find**.
Unlike ordinary geocaching, a find can be **confirmed by radio**: if a receiving station heard your
[APRS](../glossary.md#aprs) beacon near the cache, the find counts as radio-verified.

You need an amateur-radio callsign to take part. You do **not** need to install anything or know anything
about servers. A term you don't know is explained in a line or two in the [Glossary](../glossary.md).

## What you can do

- **Hunt.** Browse the map, pick a cache nearby and go there. The app can tell you when you are close.
- **Log.** Record the find in the app, from your radio, or offline when there is no signal.
- **Hide.** Place caches of your own: a single spot, a trail of stages, or a cache that travels with a
  beaconing station.
- **Compare.** Badges, ranks and ratings sit on the same map, next to heritage places such as summits and
  parks from award programmes.

Around the game sits **[the Shack](../shack/index.md)**: packet-radio tools in your browser for when you want
to operate.
