Third-party notices — APRScaching desktop app
=============================================

The desktop app is one executable made with `bun build --compile`. APRScaching
itself is AGPL-3.0-or-later; its source is linked from Settings -> Help &
credits and served at /.well-known/source.

The executable includes the Bun runtime (https://bun.sh, MIT). Bun statically
links JavaScriptCore and WebCore from WebKit, which are under the GNU LGPL 2.1,
and the libraries listed in BUN-LICENSE.txt beside this file, which reproduces
Bun's own LICENSE.md (https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

As the LGPL requires, you may modify JavaScriptCore and relink: the patched
WebKit source Bun uses is at https://github.com/oven-sh/WebKit, Bun's source is
at https://github.com/oven-sh/bun, and BUN-LICENSE.txt gives the steps to
rebuild Bun with a changed WebKit. APRScaching's own code, which `bun build
--compile` appends to that runtime, is at the Source link above; rebuilding the
executable with a relinked Bun is `bash deploy/desktop/build-exe.sh` run with
that Bun on PATH.

The soundcard modem, where the executable carries it, is Direwolf by John
Langner, WB2OSZ (https://github.com/wb2osz/direwolf, GPL-2.0-or-later), compiled to WebAssembly
in modems/direwolf and conveyed with APRScaching under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Its
complete corresponding source, the vendored Direwolf files, the glue code and
the pinned build script, is in modems/direwolf/ of the release's source archive
(aprscaching-<tag>-source.tar.gz, attached to every release) and at the Source
link; modems/direwolf/NOTICE gives the details.

The web app the executable serves is built from apps/web; the copyright notices
and licence texts of the libraries compiled into it are at
/third-party-notices.txt in the running app (Settings -> Help & credits ->
third-party notices).
